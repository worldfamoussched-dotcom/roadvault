# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

RoadVault is for creators and creative teams who move with small,
mission-critical gear: DJs, producers, musicians, photographers,
videographers, touring artists, engineers, studio teams, and agencies.

They are usually working between studios, venues, airports, hotel rooms, green
rooms, shoots, and client handoffs. Their job is not just to avoid losing
objects; it is to protect the music, footage, sessions, sets, edits, and
unreleased work stored inside those objects.

## Product Purpose

RoadVault validates a trackable lost-gear recovery ecosystem for creator gear.
The immediate product is the RoadVault Founder Kit: a physical,
tracker-compatible recovery kit that can ship without manufacturing electronics.
The future flagship is SessionVault Smart SSD: a rugged encrypted drive with
recovery intelligence.

The landing page should prove which product people want first, what price they
will tolerate, and whether the ecosystem concept is strong enough for a
crowdfunding campaign.

## Brand Personality

Premium, practical, and road-worn.

RoadVault should feel like a serious piece of creator infrastructure: durable,
useful, calm under pressure, and made for people who know what it feels like to
lose work. It should not feel cute, gimmicky, gadgety, or like a generic tracker
startup.

## Anti-references

Avoid luggage-tracker positioning, playful gadget branding, generic SaaS
landing pages, crypto-style neon tech, over-polished luxury with no utility,
and any page that makes RoadVault feel like an accessory instead of protection
for creative work.

Do not overpromise custom electronics, app functionality, Apple Find My
certification, Android Find Hub certification, or manufacturing readiness for
the Smart SSD before those paths are real.

## Design Principles

1. Make the work feel heavier than the object. A USB or SD card is small, but
   what it carries can be career-level.
2. Sell recovery, not tracking. Tracking is only one layer; identity, contact,
   reward, labeling, and return flow are the ecosystem.
3. Validate before manufacturing. The Founder Kit should feel ready and
   shippable; SessionVault should feel ambitious but clearly future-facing.
4. Use creator-specific context. DJ booths, studios, airports, green rooms,
   shoots, and handoffs should shape the language and flow.
5. Keep trust above hype. Premium utility beats clever gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public launch. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
clear form labels, reduced-motion preferences, high text contrast, and mobile
touch targets large enough for fast creator traffic from social platforms.
