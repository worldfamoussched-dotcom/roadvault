03 / Problem
Field observations
Problem

Creators lose the irreplaceable.

Every shoot ends with someone counting cards in a parking lot. We've watched it happen.

  1. FIELD-01 A working creator carries between 8 and 14 small, high-value, irreplaceable items on every shoot — SD cards, USB drives, NFC dongles, dongles for the dongles. 8–14 items
  2. FIELD-02 The category solution is hard cases built for divers and soldiers, not creators. They protect physically, but solve nothing for traceability or chain-of-custody. Pelican-era
  3. FIELD-03 When something goes missing on set, the cost isn't the device — it's the unrecoverable footage and the day's labor. We hear the same two stories at every wrap. $0 device · $$$ shoot
  4. FIELD-04 Trackers exist. Cases exist. Labels exist. Nobody has stitched them into a single coherent kit a creator can actually adopt without becoming an inventory clerk. Fragmented stack

06 / Why now
Market alignment
Why now

Three forces, one window.

Trackers became consumer infrastructure. Creator volume kept compounding. Nobody bridged the two with anything you'd actually buy.

01 — TRACKER UBIQUITY

Find My is now infrastructure.

AirTag shipped 100M+ units. Find My is opted-in by default. The tag-in-everything pattern is normalized — but no one is making the everything for creators.

02 — CREATOR ECONOMY

The customer kept growing.

Solo creator output doubled in five years and showed no sign of slowing. Their gear stack grew faster than the tools that protect it.

03 — POST-PELICAN

Hard cases stopped being enough.

Physical protection is solved. Recoverability and chain-of-custody aren't. The next category isn't tougher cases — it's traceable cases.
