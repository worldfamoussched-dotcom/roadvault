02 / WHY THIS EXISTS

Creators already travel with their livelihoods.

Hard cases protect impact. Trackers locate bags. RoadVault sits in the missing layer between them: the small, high-value objects inside the bag that actually carry the work.

Field. 01 8 to 14 small, critical items.

That is the average chaos load in a real creator bag on a travel day.

Field. 02 Recovery beats re-organization.

The customer pain is not neatness. It is loss, handoff failure, and delay.

Field. 03 V1 stays honest.

Tracker-compatible, not tracker-manufactured. No overclaim, no vapor.
