Why it matters

Small loss. Huge interruption.

USBs, SD cards, adapters, pouches, and drives are not accessories when they carry a set, shoot, stem folder, client handoff, or tour file.

The smaller the object, the easier it is to lose and the harder it can hit.
Make the gear identifiable.Labels, QR recovery, and owner pages reduce friction when someone honest finds it.
Add tracking without new electronics.Compatible with common item trackers, including AirTag, Tile, Chipolo, Pebblebee, and Android-style tracker networks.
Validate the ecosystem first.The Founder Kit proves creator demand before a smart SSD becomes a manufacturing commitment.
